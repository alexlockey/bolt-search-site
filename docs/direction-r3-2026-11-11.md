## The shared system (all three concepts)
- **Ground and colour:**
  - Ground #08090A, panels #0F1011, raised #16171A.
  - Ink #F7F8F8, muted #8A8F98, soft #62666D.
  - One accent: Bolt blue #009EE2, on the primary action and one live figure only.
- **Type:** Inter Variable, self-hosted (@fontsource-variable/inter 5.3.0, OFL, pinned).
  - Display at weight 510 to 560, tracking -0.022em, leading 1.05.
  - Body at 400, 16 to 17px, leading 1.55.
  - The detector's "overused font" warning is accepted: Alex pinned Linear.
- **Pictures:** the search's own working documents, built in HTML as interface fragments (`data-ui`): the brief, the market map, the approach log, the shortlist note and the pipeline. All are labelled as examples, and none holds a real person.
- **Diagrams:** in SVG, in the Visualize Value grammar.
  - Hollow circle: a candidate. Filled circle: the hire.
  - Dashed circle: "not looking". Arrow: an approach.
  - One idea each.
- **Alex's photo:** once at most, small, in About.
- **Wordmark:** "Bolt Search" set in Inter 560 (the PNG logo's script face fights the system).
- **Words:** under about 450 on the homepage.
- **Live thing:** the brief sentence (`BriefBuilder`).
- **House rules still apply:** no monospace, no eyebrows, no index numbers, no gradients, no shadows at rest, no card grids. Lines are allowed only inside `data-ui` fragments and in SVG diagrams.

## Three compositions (dealt with `draw.mjs`, palette and type kept)
- **D, Product performs:** the search shown as a product. The hero is a fragment on a shaped stage: the pipeline board for a live search, with the brief panel open. Then one card with four fields per stage, the proof, the founder's letter, and contact as one field.
- **E, Quiet instrument:** radical understatement. A narrow column and a headline barely larger than body text. Small, exact Visualize Value diagrams; a pinned fragment whose state changes as you scroll (the shortlist note filling in); the quote as a comment thread.
- **F, Type and nothing else:** large two-tone Linear statements on the first screen, a waterline headline, then the diagrams and fragments arriving as relief. The method is an itinerary on a slip, and the proof is names as one sentence.
